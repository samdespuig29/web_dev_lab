Chicken Pastil Recipe

Chicken Pastil is the one of my favorite dishes that i made
right now i giving you this recipe so that you can make it too

Ingredients

  • 1 kilo chicken Breast
  • 2 Tbsp of vinegar
  • 1/2 cup of soy sauce
  • 2 Tbsp Of oyster sause
  • Magic sarap
  • 1 Tbsp of sugar
  • 4 cup of cooking oil
  • 1/2 cup of garlic
  • 1/2 cup of onion
  • 4 pcs of chili
  • 2 Tbsp Of butter or Margarine
  • banana leaves for packaging
  • bay leaves
  • Lemongrass

Step 1 Ready all the ingredients And the utensils

Step 2 Chop all the ingredients

Step 3 Wash the chicekn Breast Thoroughy and put it in the
caserole a add the onion, ginger , garlic , lemongrass and litte
bit of salt and to be boil for 20 minutes

Step 4 after the chicken boil rest it for the while when the
chicken is warn strip/shredded it in smnall pieces

Step 5 Saute the onion, ginger , garlic , chili with butter or margarine in the pan

Step 6 When the aromatics had been saute add the shredded
chicken Breast saute it for the while until the flavor of aromatics is infused

Step 7 After the chicken and the aromatics is infused add the soysause,
oyster sause, bay leaves and small amount of magic sarap to taste stir it all the time
cook it for 30 minutes and serve it rice in the banana leaves
